01 / THE PROBLEM

Not every dangerous page looks dangerous.

Fake opportunities and misleading payment prompts can look convincing. A single page can combine urgency, sensitive-data requests and an unexpected fee.

ClickShield brings those signals into focus, so users can decide with context rather than pressure.
